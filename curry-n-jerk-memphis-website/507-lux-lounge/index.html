Curry N Jerk brings Panamanian and Jamaican culinary influences together in downtown Memphis. The menu moves from Panamanian pasta and fried rice to smoky jerk, slow-simmered curry and island-inspired takes on Southern favorites.

The common thread is generous food, layered seasoning and hospitality that makes the table feel a little warmer.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Planning dinner, choosing a dish, or ordering for a group? Start here.

What type of food does Curry N Jerk serve?+

Curry N Jerk is a full-service Caribbean restaurant serving Panamanian and Jamaican dishes, including oxtails, jerk chicken, curry chicken, curry goat, Panamanian pasta, seafood, plant-based dishes and island-inspired cocktails.

Where is Curry N Jerk located?+

Curry N Jerk is at 150 Monroe Avenue in downtown Memphis, near The Peabody hotel and AutoZone Park.

Where do I park if I come to Curry N Jerk?+

Free street parking is available nearby. Premium Parking P2564 is directly across the street from Curry N Jerk. Premium Parking P2720, located at 21 S. Second St., Memphis, TN 38103, is about a two-minute walk from the restaurant.

What dishes is Curry N Jerk known for?+

Popular choices include oxtails, jerk chicken, curry chicken, the Curry Chicken and Jerk Chicken combination, curry goat, Panamanian pasta, Curry Shrimp and Grits, and Panamanian Fried Rice.

Does Curry N Jerk serve oxtails?+

Yes. Oxtails are a signature dish and are offered as a plate and in select combination meals. Current choices and prices are on the Toast ordering menu.

Does Curry N Jerk offer vegan or vegetarian food?+

Yes. The current menu includes a Vegan Vegetable Medley, Vegan Jerk Veggie Fried Rice, Vegan Jerk Burger and Vegetarian Panamanian Pasta.

Can I order Curry N Jerk for pickup or delivery?+

Yes. Select Order Now to order pickup or delivery directly through Curry N Jerk’s Toast menu.

Does Curry N Jerk charge gratuity?+

Yes. A 20% gratuity is added to all dine-in guest checks, and a 15% gratuity is added to all takeout (to-go) orders.

Does Curry N Jerk offer catering?+

Yes. Curry N Jerk welcomes catering inquiries for corporate lunches, meetings, weddings, reunions, celebrations and group events in Memphis.

Does Curry N Jerk have happy hour?+

Yes. The currently published happy hour is Tuesday–Saturday, 4:00–7:00 PM. Specials may change; check the Happy Hour page before visiting.
