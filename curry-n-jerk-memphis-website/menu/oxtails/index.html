A closer look at Oxtails Plate.

This is a rich, slow-cooked Caribbean plate for guests who want deep savory flavor and tender oxtails. It is one of the clearest introductions to the comfort-food side of the Curry N Jerk menu.

If you are looking specifically for oxtails in Memphis, start here. The full plate is available at Curry N Jerk in downtown Memphis for dine-in and through the restaurant’s direct Toast ordering menu.
