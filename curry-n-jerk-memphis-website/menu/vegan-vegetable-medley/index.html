What to expect

A closer look at Vegan Vegetable Medley.

This plate keeps vegetables at the center rather than treating them like an afterthought. The combination of colorful produce, aromatic jerk seasoning and sweet plantains delivers a complete plant-based Caribbean option.

Choose the Vegan Vegetable Medley when you want vegan Caribbean food in downtown Memphis with vegetables, seasoning and contrast in every bite. Guests with allergies should discuss preparation and possible cross-contact with the restaurant team.
