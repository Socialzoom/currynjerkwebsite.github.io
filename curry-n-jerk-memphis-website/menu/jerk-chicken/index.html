What to expect

A closer look at Jerk Chicken Plate.

Jerk is a Caribbean seasoning and cooking tradition known for aromatic spice, heat and smoke. Curry N Jerk’s plate balances that smoky grilled character with two familiar Caribbean sides.

This is a strong first order for guests searching for jerk chicken in Memphis or anyone who wants grilled flavor and a more spirited level of seasoning. Ask the restaurant team about current heat and sauce options.
