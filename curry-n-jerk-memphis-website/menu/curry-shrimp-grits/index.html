What to expect

A closer look at Curry Shrimp & Grits.

This dish places Caribbean curry beside a Southern comfort-food staple. The result is a Curry N Jerk specialty that connects the restaurant’s island flavors directly to its home in Memphis.

Choose Curry Shrimp & Grits when you want seafood, creamy grits and curry in the same bowl. It is an approachable bridge for Memphis diners who know shrimp and grits but want to experience it through a Caribbean lens.

Find it in downtown Memphis

Curry N Jerk serves this dish at 150 Monroe Avenue in downtown Memphis, near The Peabody, AutoZone Park, Beale Street, FedExForum and the Orpheum Theatre.
