A closer look at Vegetarian Panamanian Pasta.

This dish brings the Panamanian side of Curry N Jerk’s menu into a vegetarian format. It offers the comfort of pasta while keeping vegetables at the center of the plate.

Choose Vegetarian Panamanian Pasta when you want a meat-free entrée with a distinct place in the restaurant’s Panamanian identity. Ask the team about ingredients and preparation when managing allergies or other dietary needs.
