Know before you order

Vegan and vegetarian, clearly marked.

Vegan selections

The Vegan Vegetable Medley, Vegan Jerk Veggie Fried Rice, Vegan Jerk Burger and Vegan “Chicken” Drumsticks & Plantains are listed as plant-based choices.

Vegetarian selections

The Vegetarian Panamanian Pasta and Vegetarian Jerk Alfredo Pasta are meat-free choices. The Alfredo contains dairy and is not vegan.

Allergy or dietary concern? Please speak with the Curry N Jerk team before ordering so ingredients, preparation and potential cross-contact can be discussed.
