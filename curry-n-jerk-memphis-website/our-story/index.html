Our table

Panama. Jamaica. Memphis.

Curry N Jerk is a full-service Caribbean restaurant in downtown Memphis specializing in Panamanian and Jamaican dishes, including oxtails, jerk chicken, curry chicken, Panamanian pasta and island-inspired specialties.

What makes us Curry N Jerk

Two culinary traditions, one generous table.

On one side of the name is curry: aromatic, slow-simmered dishes like curry chicken and curry goat. On the other is jerk: boldly seasoned, smoky chicken, wings and seafood. Panamanian pasta, fried rice and yellow rice widen the table and make the menu distinctly Curry N Jerk.

In downtown Memphis, those influences meet local dining traditions in dishes like Curry Shrimp and Grits and Salmon and Grits. The result is Caribbean food that can feel familiar on the first bite and still offer something new.

Hospitality in the heart of downtown

Curry N Jerk welcomes Memphis residents, downtown professionals, visiting families, tourists and event guests for a full-service dinner experience, direct pickup and delivery, and catering.
