Our commitment

Accessibility Statement

Curry N Jerk is committed to making its website and restaurant information usable by as many people as possible.

Digital accessibility

This site is designed toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA with keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, labeled controls, descriptive image text, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts.

Need assistance?

If you have difficulty using this website or need restaurant information in another format, call (901) 480-8381 or email cnjowner@outlook.com. Please describe the page and the assistance you need.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Curry N Jerk welcomes feedback and will review reported barriers.
