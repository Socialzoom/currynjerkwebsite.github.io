Website information

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Curry N Jerk website handles inquiry information, analytics and links to external services.

Information you choose to provide

When you contact Curry N Jerk, you may provide your name, contact information and event details. Curry N Jerk uses that information to respond to the inquiry and provide requested services.

Analytics

The site is prepared to record privacy-conscious events such as order-button clicks, menu views, direction requests, phone clicks and catering-inquiry activity. Analytics identifiers must be configured before custom-domain launch, and consent controls must be added if legally required for the selected tools.

External services

Online ordering takes place on Toast. Directions open Google Maps. Catering inquiry submissions are processed by FormSubmit and delivered by email to Curry N Jerk. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to cnjowner@outlook.com.
